Revision booklet · for the printed-notes exam

Print this and take it into the exam

The booklet is 44 pages: A4 landscape, three columns, every part starting on a fresh page (22 sheets printed double-sided). It opens with a contents list, a premises page (every problem type: what you are given, what you must find) and an “if the question says…” index. Then come the answer recipes, the maths basics (skew matrix, cross product, s = ut + ½at², atan2…) and the standard robots (RR, RP, RRP, SCARA, Puma, Panda: tables, ETS, full matrices). After those, W1–W12 follow the slides’ order. Each week has formulas with a symbols table, figures cut from the slides, full derivations, steps, alternative approaches side by side, controller block diagrams (model-based in orange, servo in blue) and solved BFS, DFS, Dijkstra and A* tables, and ends with exam-style questions solved in full. The W13 sample questions, solved, are at the back.

Colours: blue formula, green steps, purple worked example, grey derivation, teal alternatives, red dashed trap, amber slide check, slate premise, black exam question. The tab on each page’s right edge shows the section.

How to use it in the exam
  1. Find the question type on the premises page or in the index (“derive the inverse kinematics”, “mobility”, “drive a circle”…).
  2. Write down the given and the find, then follow the steps box on the week page; its worked example has the same shape.
  3. Stuck? Each week’s last pages are exam-style questions solved in full; the W13 sample answers are at the back.
