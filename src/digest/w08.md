## Formulas
**F1 Recap (p. 8; source: slide image).**
$$ {}^0\mathbf T_n\ \text{or}\ \boldsymbol\xi_n=\mathcal K(\mathbf q),\ \ \mathbf q=\{q_i,\ i\in[1,\dots,n]\};\quad \mathbf q=\mathcal K^{-1}(\boldsymbol\xi_n);\quad {}^0\boldsymbol\nu={}^0\mathbf J(\mathbf q)\dot{\mathbf q};\quad \mathbf Q=\mathbf M(\mathbf q)\ddot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf C(\mathbf q,\dot{\mathbf q})+\boldsymbol g(\mathbf q)$$
q joint coordinates; ν spatial velocity; J Jacobian; Q joint torques/forces; M inertia matrix; C Coriolis/centripetal; g gravity vector. Also trajectory generation gives q(t), ξ_n(t).

**F2 Servo error (p. 13; image p. 39).**
$$E=\mathbf q_d-\mathbf q\ \ (\text{single-joint form } E=x_d-x),\quad \dot E=\dot x_d-\dot x$$
Subscript d = desired. Goal: E → 0 (or small) quickly as time passes.

**F3 Spring-mass-damper (pp. 16–17; image p. 17).** m mass, b damping coefficient, k stiffness, x position of block.
$$m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=0;\quad \text{characteristic eq. } ms^2+bs+k=0;\quad s_{1,2}=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4mk}}{2m}$$
Slide calls this a second-order, homogeneous, linear ODE with constant coefficients; the roots are the **poles**.

**F4 Case 1, real unequal roots (p. 18).** $x(t)=A_1e^{s_1t}+A_2e^{s_2t}$; A₁, A₂ from x(0), ẋ(0).

**F5 Case 2, complex roots (p. 21; image).** $s_{1,2}=\lambda\pm i\mu$, $x(t)=A_1e^{\lambda t}\cos(\mu t)+A_2e^{\lambda t}\sin(\mu t)$.
(derived, not on slide: λ = −b/2m, μ = √(4mk−b²)/2m.)

**F6 Case 3, real equal roots (p. 24; image).** $s_1=s_2=-\dfrac{b}{2m}$, $x(t)=(A_1+A_2t)e^{-\frac{b}{2m}t}$.

**F7 Actuated system (p. 28).** $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=f$, f = actuator force.

**F8 PD control law (p. 29, 30; image p. 30).** $f=-k_px-k_v\dot x$ (needs measured x and ẋ). k_p position gain, k_v velocity gain.

**F9 Closed-loop with PD (p. 32; image).**
$$m\ddot x+(b+k_v)\dot x+(k+k_p)x=0\ \Rightarrow\ m\ddot x+b'\dot x+k'x=0,\ b'=b+k_v,\ k'=k+k_p$$
Slide: critically damped if $b'^2=4mk'$ (prime/acute accent on b, k on image; hand-annotated).

**F10 Partitioning (pp. 33–34; image).** $f=\alpha f'+\beta$; α, β functions or constants chosen so f′ is a new input and the system appears to be **unit mass**. Choose $\alpha=m$, $\beta=b\dot x+kx$ ⇒ $\ddot x=f'$. Model-based part = α, β (uses m, b, k); servo part = the f′ law.

**F11 Servo law, regulation (p. 36; image).** $f'=-k_v\dot x-k_p x$ ⇒ $\ddot x+k_v\dot x+k_px=0$ (hand-note: $s^2+k_vs+k_p=0$). "Independent of m, b, k." Slide states critical damping as **k_p² = 4k_v — see Uncertain U1**.

**F12 Trajectory following (p. 39; image).** $x_d(t)$ smooth and twice differentiable (so ẋ_d, ẍ_d exist).
$$f'=\ddot x_d+k_v\dot E+k_pE\ \Rightarrow\ \ddot x=\ddot x_d+k_v\dot E+k_pE\ \Rightarrow\ 0=\ddot E+k_v\dot E+k_pE$$
"Second order in error space." Full law: $f=m f'+b\dot x+kx$ (block diagram p. 40).

**F13 Disturbance (p. 41–42; image).** Disturbance force f_disturb enters at the force summing junction (red arrow, "−" sign).
$$\ddot E+k_v\dot E+k_pE=f_{disturb}/m$$
BIBO principle: bounded f_disturb ⇒ bounded E.

**F14 Steady-state error (p. 43; image).** Constant f_disturb, set Ë = Ė = 0: $k_pE=f_{disturb}/m\Rightarrow E=\dfrac{f_{disturb}}{k_p\,m}$.

**F15 PID law (p. 44; image).** $f'=\ddot x_d+k_v\dot E+k_pE+k_i\int E\,dt$; error eq. $\ddot E+k_v\dot E+k_pE+k_i\!\int\!E\,dt=f_{disturb}/m$ (hand-note "at steady state"). k_i integral gain.

**F16 Third order (p. 45; image).** If E(t)=0 for t<0, then for t>0: $\dddot E+k_v\ddot E+k_p\dot E+k_iE=\dot f_{disturb}/m$. "Verify that at steady state E=0." (Constant f_disturb ⇒ ḟ_disturb = 0 ⇒ k_iE=0; derived.)

**F17 DC motor (pp. 48–49; image).** Electrical: armature circuit V_a, R_a, L_a, i_a, back-emf V_b; field circuit V_f, R_f, L_f, i_f "generates B". Mechanical: motor torque τ_m/Q_m, speed q̇_m, rotor inertia I_m, friction f_m.
$$Q_m(t)=k_1Bi_a=k_4i_a;\quad Q_{net}=Q_m-f_m;\quad I_m\ddot q_m=k_4i_a-c_m\dot q_m\ \ (\text{boxed})$$
c_m viscous friction coefficient of motor.

**F18 Gearing (p. 50; image).** η = gear ratio.
$$Q=\eta Q_m;\quad \dot q_{axle}=\tfrac1\eta\dot q_m;\quad Q=(I+\eta^2I_m)\ddot q_{axle}+(c+\eta^2c_m)\dot q_{axle}$$
I, c = link-side inertia and friction (axle). Reflected motor inertia/friction scale with η² (hand-annotated). (derived check: from ηQ_m=ηI_mq̈_m+ηc_mq̇_m+Q_load with q_m=ηq_axle gives the same.)

## Worked examples and numbers on the slides
All use $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=0$, x(0)=−1, ẋ(0)=0. I checked each by hand and numerically (roots via numpy); all correct.
- **p. 19 real unequal:** m=1, b=3, k=2. Roots s₁=−1, s₂=−2. x=A₁e^{−t}+A₂e^{−2t}. ICs: A₁+A₂=−1 and −A₁−2A₂=0 ⇒ A₁=−2, A₂=1. **x(t)=−2e^{−t}+e^{−2t}.** Plot p. 20: poles at −2 and −1 on real axis; x rises monotonically from −1 to 0, no overshoot, slowest of the three (settles ~t≈8–10 s).
- **p. 22 complex:** m=b=k=1. s=−1/2 ± i√3/2. x=A₁e^{−t/2}cos(√3t/2)+A₂e^{−t/2}sin(√3t/2). ICs: A₁=−1 and −A₁/2+√3A₂/2=0 ⇒ A₂=−1/√3. **x(t)=−e^{−t/2}[cos(√3t/2)+(1/√3)sin(√3t/2)].** Plot p. 23: overshoots above 0 (peak ≈+0.16 near t≈3.6 s), small undershoot, settles ~t≈10 s.
- **p. 25 real equal:** m=1, b=4, k=4. s₁=s₂=−2. x=(A₁+A₂t)e^{−2t}. ICs: A₁=−1 and −2A₁+A₂=0 ⇒ A₂=−2. **x(t)=(−1−2t)e^{−2t}.** Plot p. 26 (all three overlaid, hand-labelled): the equal-roots curve is the fastest to 0 without overshoot; "complex" overshoots; "real, unequal" is slowest. Pole plot: single × at −2.
- Slide-stated example of criticality: p. 32 "if k_p and k_v chosen such that b′²=4mk′, the system is critically damped"; p. 36 gives the unit-mass version (see U1). No numeric gain example is given on the slides.

## Conventions used
- Torque vector Q (also τ for motor torque, τ_m); joint coordinates q; desired quantities carry subscript d (or D in block diagrams on p. 14: q_D).
- E = desired − actual (signs: x_d − x). Control gains k_p, k_v, k_i all positive; feedback shown with "−" signs on p. 30.
- Gear ratio η defined so Q = ηQ_m and q̇_axle = q̇_m/η (motor is faster than axle for η>1).
- Primes/acute accent on f′ (f with acute accent), b′, k′ denote modified quantities (PD-modified or servo input). Handwritten annotations in red are in the PDF.
- Single-joint treatment: mass–spring–damper stands in for a joint; the "system" is treated as having constant m, b, k.

## Uncertain / disagreements
- **U1 (slide error).** p. 36 states critical damping as "k_p² = 4k_v" for ẍ+k_vẋ+k_px=0. Characteristic equation s²+k_vs+k_p=0 (shown handwritten on the same slide) gives **k_v²=4k_p** (and p. 32 gives b′²=4mk′, consistent with the latter). The slide has the subscripts swapped; I believe the slide is wrong. Verified: for this ODE, roots are equal iff k_v²−4k_p=0.
- U2. p. 45 condition "E(t)=0 for t<0" is stated to turn the integral equation into the derivative form; it is a loose justification (differentiating the equation with constant f_disturb does not need it). Treat the result (third-order, E=0 at steady state for constant disturbance) as the examinable point.
- U3. Symbol mixing on pp. 48–50: motor friction written f_m (torque lost), c_m (coefficient), and τ_m vs Q_m for motor torque; Q on the left of the gearing equations appears to be the motor torque transmitted to the axle (ηQ_m) in the sense of my derivation, but the slide does not say so explicitly.
