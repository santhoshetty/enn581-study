## Formulas
**Polynomial** (p. 15, cell 13): $q(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{N}a_kt^k$; order N needs N+1 conditions. Source: notebook.

**Cubic** (p. 16, cell 14): $q=a_0+a_1t+a_2t^2+a_3t^3$, $\dot q=a_1+2a_2t+3a_3t^2$, $\ddot q=2a_2+6a_3t$. Conditions $q(0)=q_0$, $q(t_F)=q_F$, $\dot q(0)=\dot q_0$, $\dot q(t_F)=\dot q_F$ (zero for rest-to-rest). Source: notebook (slide has no $\ddot q$ here). Cell 16 matrix rows: $[1,0,0,0]$, $[1,t_F,t_F^2,t_F^3]$, $[0,1,0,0]$, $[0,1,2t_F,3t_F^2]$ times $a$ = $(q_0,q_F,\dot q_0,\dot q_F)$.

**Cubic coefficients [derived; slides give only the numeric example]**, $t_0=0$:
$a_0=q_0,\ a_1=\dot q_0,\ a_2=\dfrac{3(q_F-q_0)-(2\dot q_0+\dot q_F)t_F}{t_F^2},\ a_3=\dfrac{-2(q_F-q_0)+(\dot q_0+\dot q_F)t_F}{t_F^3}$.
Rest-to-rest: $a_2=3\Delta q/t_F^2$, $a_3=-2\Delta q/t_F^3$, peak speed $1.5\Delta q/t_F$ at $t_F/2$ (matches p. 19: 30 °/s). Reproduces Example 1.

**Quintic** (p. 29, cell 25): $q=a_0+\dots+a_5t^5$; $\dot q=a_1+2a_2t+3a_3t^2+4a_4t^3+5a_5t^4$; $\ddot q=2a_2+6a_3t+12a_4t^2+20a_5t^3$. 6 conditions: $q,\dot q,\ddot q$ at $0$ and $t_F$. Source: slide text.

**Via-point velocity rule** (p. 27, cell 21): if the slopes of adjacent segments change sign, via velocity = 0; else the average of the two slopes (slope = $\Delta q/\Delta t$).

**Parabolic blend** (pp. 33–34, cell 35; source: slide image, matches notebook):
$$q_{B1}=q_0+\tfrac{a(t_B-t_0)^2}{2},\quad q_{B2}=q_{B1}+v(t_F-2t_B),\quad q_F=q_{B2}+vt_B-\tfrac{at_B^2}{2},\quad t_B=\tfrac{v}{a}$$
$q_{B1}$ position at end of first blend, $q_{B2}$ at start of last blend (time $t_F-t_B$), $v$ coast velocity, $a$ blend acceleration magnitude, $t_B$ blend duration. Slide tags: eq. 1 and 3 "second order polynomial in time", eq. 2 "linear in time". Eliminating:
$$a t_B^2-a(t_F-t_0)t_B+\big(q_F-q_0-\tfrac{a t_0^2}{2}\big)=0;\ \ t_0=0:\ a t_B^2-at_Ft_B+(q_F-q_0)=0$$
$$t_B=\frac{t_F}{2}\pm\frac{\sqrt{a^2t_F^2-4a(q_F-q_0)}}{2a},\qquad \text{solution exists iff } a\ge\frac{4(q_F-q_0)}{t_F^2}$$
Slide (p. 36) uses non-strict "≥" and says take the root positive and smaller than $t_F$ (cell 35). Motor hardware sets a maximum $a$. Equality gives $t_B=t_F/2$, no coast phase [mine].

**Multi-segment blends** (pp. 39–40, symbols only): $t_{djk}$ total duration $q_j\to q_k$; $t_{jk}$ duration of linear part; $t_j$ blend duration at $q_j$; $v_{jk}$ slope; $a_j$ blend acceleration at $q_j$. Given via points, $t_{djk}$, $a_j$; find blend times $t_k$ (Craig §7.3). Path passes near, not through, via points (cell 42: passing through all with rest at ends is over-constrained).

**Constant-speed motion on $y=mx+c$** (p. 53, image): $x(t)=x_0+\dfrac{vt}{\sqrt{1+m^2}}$, $y(t)=y_0+\dfrac{vmt}{\sqrt{1+m^2}}$.

**2R IK** (p. 56, image; cell 55): $\cos q_2=\dfrac{x^2+y^2-L_1^2-L_2^2}{2L_1L_2}$, $\sin q_2=\pm\sqrt{1-\cos^2q_2}$, $q_2=\mathrm{atan2}(\sin q_2,\cos q_2)$, $q_1=\mathrm{atan2}(y,x)-\mathrm{atan2}(L_2\sin q_2,\ L_1+L_2\cos q_2)$. The notebook uses the + root only. Slide: if speed is not constant, get joint velocity via the Jacobian. 2R FK (cell 59): $x=L_1c_1+L_2c_{12}$, $y=L_1s_1+L_2s_{12}$.
